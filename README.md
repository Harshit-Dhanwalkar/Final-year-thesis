# Dataset

## [ERA5 hourly data on single levels from 1940 to present](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels?tab=overview)

Documentation : https://confluence.ecmwf.int/display/CKB/ERA5%3A+data+documentation

## Dataset bounds

Latitude = [7, 30] degrees
Longitude = [70, 95] degrees
Time = [June, July, August, September]

### Variables:

1. Mean Sea Level Pressure (`msl`)
2. Surface Pressure (`sp`)
3. 10m U-Component of Wind (`u10`)
4. 10m V-Component of Wind (`v10`)
5. Mean Surface Latent Heat Flux (`avg_slhf`)
6. Mean Surface Sensible Heat Flux (`avg_ishf`)
7. Total Precipitation (`tp`)
8. Soil Temperature Level 1 (`stl1`)
9. Volumetric Soil Water Layer 1 (`swvl1`)
10. 2m Temperature (`t2m`)
11. 2m Dew Point Temperature (`d2m`)

### Constants:

1. Land-sea mask (`lsm`)
2. Geopotential (`z`)
3. Soil Type (`slt`)
